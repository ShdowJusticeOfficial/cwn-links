WHY CWN EXISTS

Our Mission

Community Watch Network exists to help create safer, better-informed and more accountable online communities. CWN supports people affected by online harm, develops practical safety resources and works with communities to identify risks, document concerns and encourage responsible action.

🛡️

Protect Communities

Help communities identify online risks, improve safety practices and respond appropriately to credible concerns.

📚

Educate and Inform

Publish accessible online-safety guidance, awareness resources and information that helps people make safer decisions.

⚖️

Act Responsibly

Handle reports and evidence carefully, remain impartial and encourage proportionate action through appropriate platforms and authorities.

Important: CWN is an independent community organisation. It is not a police force, government agency, emergency service, court, antivirus company or substitute for qualified legal, safeguarding or cybersecurity professionals.

EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

Community Watch Network Executive Leadership

Executive Leadership is the highest level of authority within Community Watch Network.

The Founder, Co-Founder / Second-in-Command and Third-in-Command establish strategic direction, supervise all Directorates and ensure CWN operates professionally, effectively and in accordance with its mission.

Each Executive Leadership position may appoint a Deputy to assist with day-to-day responsibilities and provide continuity when required. All current Executive Leadership Deputy positions are vacant.

ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE

Community Watch Network Directorates

CWN Directorates are responsible for overseeing their respective departments and maintaining professionalism, accountability and operational effectiveness across the organisation.

Each Director reports to Executive Leadership and is responsible for the management, development and performance of their department.

DEPUTY DIRECTORS

Deputy Director Responsibilities

  • Assists the Director with day-to-day responsibilities.
  • Acts on behalf of the Director during periods of absence.
  • Supports planning, organisation and departmental management.
  • Helps maintain departmental standards and continuity.
  • Is expected to be capable of assuming the Director role if required.
LEADERSHIP STANDARDS

Director Expectations

  • Demonstrate professionalism and maturity.
  • Maintain activity within their department.
  • Lead by example and uphold CWN standards.
  • Work collaboratively with other Directorates.
  • Submit reports or updates to Executive Leadership when required.
FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

Additional Directorates

Executive Leadership may establish, merge, rename or dissolve Directorates as Community Watch Network grows and operational requirements evolve.
