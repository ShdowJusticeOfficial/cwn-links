Data & Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information the Community Watch Network links page and local File Risk Scanner collect, what they do not collect, and how external infrastructure may process limited technical information.

Effective: 3 August 2026
Scope: this notice covers the public CWN links page at cwn-links.vercel.app and its browser-based File Risk Scanner. Other CWN services, forms, Discord servers, help centres or investigation systems may require separate privacy information.

1. Who is responsible for this page?

Community Watch Network is responsible for the content and functionality published on this links page.

Privacy enquiries can be sent to: Communityshieldofficial@gmail.com

2. What this page intentionally collects

General page visits CWN does not currently operate its own account system, database, advertising tracker or custom visitor analytics on this static links page.
File scanner uploads None. Files selected in the File Risk Scanner are analysed locally by JavaScript inside the visitor’s browser.
File contents CWN does not receive or store the contents of files selected in the local scanner.
Filenames and hashes The scanner does not transmit selected filenames or calculated SHA-256 hashes to CWN. They remain within the current browser session unless the visitor copies or saves them independently.
Cookies set by CWN code The current links-page code does not intentionally set advertising, analytics or profiling cookies.
Direct communications When a person voluntarily emails CWN, the email address, message content and information they choose to provide are received through the relevant email service.
Public staff information The directory publishes organisational role information, display names and Discord user IDs supplied or approved for public verification purposes.

3. Hosting and technical logs

This website is hosted using Vercel. Like most hosting and security providers, Vercel may process technical request information required to deliver and protect the website. This may include information such as an IP address, request time, requested page, browser or device information, and security or error logs.

This infrastructure processing is separate from the local File Risk Scanner. Selecting or scanning a file does not cause the file to be sent to the web server.

4. Why information may be used

  • To provide and secure the website.
  • To respond to messages or support enquiries voluntarily sent to CWN.
  • To publish and verify CWN organisational roles.
  • To investigate technical faults, abuse or security incidents.
  • To meet legal, safeguarding or regulatory obligations where applicable.

5. Information CWN does not use this page for

  • Selling visitor information.
  • Behavioural advertising.
  • Automated profiling or eligibility decisions.
  • Uploading files selected in the local scanner.
  • Collecting passwords, payment-card details or government identification through the links page.
  • Secretly activating cameras, microphones or location services.

6. Retention

The local File Risk Scanner does not retain selected files after the visitor clears the file, reloads the page or closes the browser tab.

Direct emails and related records should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, protect users, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations.

Hosting and security-log retention is controlled in part by the hosting provider and the configuration of the CWN Vercel account.

7. Sharing and disclosure

CWN does not sell information collected through this page. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the site or communications, such as Vercel and the relevant email provider.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect a person, investigate abuse, comply with law, respond to lawful requests or refer a serious concern to an appropriate platform, safeguarding body or authority.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including asking for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or information about how it is used.

Requests should be sent to Communityshieldofficial@gmail.com . CWN may need enough information to verify the request and locate the relevant records.

9. File Risk Scanner limitations

The scanner checks limited indicators such as filename patterns, file extensions, basic signatures and a locally calculated SHA-256 hash. It does not execute files, unpack every archive, inspect all file content, connect to a commercial malware database or guarantee that a file is safe.

Visitors should use reputable, updated antivirus or endpoint security software and avoid opening files from untrusted sources.

Internal privacy and security alerts

CWN Shield may send an internal alert to an access-restricted CWN Discord channel when a submission contains blocked credentials, confirmed personal information, or an apparent attempt to bypass the personal-information protection check.

These alerts include finding categories, report metadata and security identifiers. They are designed not to reproduce the detected personal information, credential or secret value.

Access to these alerts should be limited to authorised CWN personnel who require the information for privacy, security, safeguarding or technical review.

Personal-information protection

The CWN bug-report form performs local pattern matching before submission to identify information that may unintentionally identify a person or expose an account credential. This may include email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, postal codes, coordinates, access tokens, API credentials and private keys.

The browser check does not send detected values to a separate analysis service. The report is checked again by the CWN server-side endpoint to prevent the browser protection from being bypassed.

High-risk credentials and private keys are rejected. Other possible personal information must be redacted or explicitly confirmed before submission. Detection is based on patterns and may produce false positives or fail to recognise unusual formats.

Human verification and abuse prevention

The CWN bug-report form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate visitors from automated abuse. Cloudflare processes limited technical and interaction information required to issue and validate the verification token.

The generated token is submitted to CWN’s server-side bug-report endpoint and then validated with Cloudflare. The token is used for security and anti-spam purposes and is not used by CWN for advertising.

Visitors should review Cloudflare’s applicable privacy information for further details about its processing.

Bug report submissions

When a visitor submits the CWN bug-report form, CWN receives the information entered into the form. This may include the reporter’s display name, optional contact information, bug description, reproduction steps, device details, affected URLs and evidence links.

The report is transmitted to a restricted CWN Discord logging channel using a server-side webhook. The webhook credential is not exposed to the visitor’s browser.

Technical connection information may be processed temporarily for security and rate-limiting purposes. CWN uses a shortened, one-way identifier for this protection rather than publishing the visitor’s IP address in the Discord report.

Bug-report information should be retained only for as long as needed to investigate, resolve and document the issue, protect the service, or meet applicable legal obligations.

10. Changes to this notice

CWN may update this notice when the website, scanner, hosting, analytics, forms or data-handling practices change. The effective date at the top should be updated whenever a material change is published.
