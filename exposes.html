PROCESS

How an investigation reaches publication

Publication should occur only after appropriate review and verification.

  1. 01
    Report received

    CWN receives a report or identifies a public-interest concern.

  2. 02
    Initial assessment

    Staff assess scope, safety, relevance and available information.

  3. 03
    Evidence collection

    Relevant material is collected, organised and assigned evidence references.

  4. 04
    Verification

    Claims, dates, sources and evidence are reviewed for accuracy and context.

  5. 05
    Right of reply

    Where appropriate, relevant subjects are invited to provide a response.

  6. 06
    Publication and updates

    The report is published with corrections and update history retained.

PUBLICATION STANDARDS

Before you read

Allegations are identified

Unverified claims must not be presented as established fact.

Responses are preserved

Relevant replies, denials and clarifications should be shown fairly.

Corrections remain visible

Material changes should be recorded in an update history.

Private information is limited

Personal information should only be included where necessary, lawful and proportionate.
